ACT/SAT SUPPORT

All of our tutors have earned ACT scores of 34 and above and/or SAT scores of 750 and above and have access to a large bank of tutoring resources. Currently, we offer tutoring services for all sections of the ACT, SAT, and PSAT. All sessions are 60 minutes long and scheduled via Calendly. Students may sign up for a maximum of two sessions of SAT/ACT tutoring per week across all tutors
